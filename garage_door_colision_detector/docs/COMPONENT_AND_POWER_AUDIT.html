What changed

The native 00_Mounting_Overview.SchDoc now shows receivers/controller on wall A, powered emitters on wall B, and two external AC/DC adapters. The former passive-reflector arrangement has been removed from this page. The native electrical sheets and PCB were not rebuilt in this update.

PS1 and PS2: MEAN WELL GST25E12-P1J, each 12 V DC, 2.08 A, EU plug-in adapter, nominal body 79 × 54 × 33 mm. This body size excludes the installed socket, projecting AC pins, DC cable bends and service clearance. Use intact adapters at suitable outlets outside the optical enclosures. No bare mains converter or mains traces on the custom PCB.

P1J output: 5.5 × 2.1 mm, centre positive. A matching insulated panel jack connects by a harness to the existing power terminal. The exact jack remains unselected; do not drill its mounting hole yet. The two DC outputs are not joined. Controller power goes through protection and the existing 5 V buck; the emitter side needs separately implemented protected distribution.

Do not assume generic marketplace E3Z-labelled parts match Omron optical performance, dimensions or approvals. No manufacturer STEP was downloaded or linked for these candidates in this update.

Dimensions that need resolving

The earlier arrangement used 100 mm vertical optical-axis pitch. The latest message says 10 mm between sensors. If that means 10 mm centre-to-centre, the proposed Omron bodies cannot simply be stacked in their usual orientation: their height is 31 mm. If it means a 10 mm body-edge gap, pitch is different again. If it means 10 mm clearance from the door, it is not sensor pitch.

Do not release the previous 100 × 100 × 300 mm box or PCB outline until this is clarified. Keep optical heads on their own rigid adjustable rail, separately from a compact controller PCB. A schematic's symbol size is never a mechanical dimension.

Altium outside-sheet background
