Verification and recovery

Native repair log: hardware/altium/PinRepair.audit.txt. The script verifies pad counts and net assignments for all 15 added parts and compares every schematic pin's position/orientation before and after editing. Backups: hardware/altium/History/pin-repair-20260910.

Use the project in hardware_projects, not the old project folder. Saving a schematic alone does not synchronize the PCB. Use Design > Update PCB Document on the controller PCB and inspect a fresh ECO.

This repair is not a fabrication release. Complete placement, routing, ERC/DRC, remaining exact 3D models and optical acceptance testing before ordering or relying on the detector. Do not rerun RepairMissingPins: its guards intentionally refuse overwriting the repair library or duplicating parts.
