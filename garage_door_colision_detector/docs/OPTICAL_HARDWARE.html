Emitter / receiver hardware

Native Altium update · 8 September 2026 · advisory detector, not a certified safety device.

Separate powered heads face each other across the approximately 2.4 m opening. Fix them to stationary uprights using adjustable brackets, clear of the complete door/roller travel envelope. Never drill spring, cable or other load-bearing door hardware without the door manufacturer's approval. Electronics stay in a separate compact box; no 300–600 mm PCB is necessary.

What is a component now?

04_Optical_Heads.SchDoc contains native editable TX/RX components, with real Omron part numbers, cable colours, dimensions and ordering information. They are Mechanical BOM components because they are external wired devices, not PCB-mounted parts. Their optical symbol is a functional representation, not a drawing to scale. The connector symbols in sheet 02 represent the PCB terminals only.

lib/OpticalHardware.SchLib contains the reusable emitter, receiver and connector symbols. lib/OpticalHardware.PcbLib contains the Phoenix 1715734 footprint with embedded, matching KiCad STEP. J2/J3/J4/J6 use that footprint. Native components are not automatically manufacturer-approved libraries; source and verification status are recorded explicitly.

Remaining limitations: Omron's STEP server refused the download, including its direct link from DigiKey. The sensor STEP is not included and no invented body replaces it. Other legacy PCB components still require footprint/model repair, schematic synchronization and routing. Do not order the PCB from this draft. The exact optical boundary and cross-talk performance also remain untested in the garage.

Saved-data verification passed: 117 existing schematic connectivity assertions; four terminal footprints, twelve pad/net/drill assignments and four 3D body objects. Wiring

LocationConnection
Each emitterBrown → fused TX +12 V; blue → TX 0 V. Parallel DC distribution to the other emitters. No signal wire or second ESP32.
Each receiverBrown → terminal 1 (+12V_SENSOR); blue → terminal 2 (GND); black → terminal 3 (BEAMn_SIG).
Channel mappingRX1→J2, RX2→J3, RX3→J4, RX4→J6. Configure only installed channels.
Receiver selectorLIGHT-ON: receiving light sinks current. Loss of light, receiver power or cable continuity deasserts clear. Dark-ON is the wrong mode for this interface.

The two isolated adapters do not need their DC grounds joined across the garage. Receiver output never goes directly to an ESP32 pin: retain the existing current-limited optocoupler input stage. Mechanical component pins document off-board wiring; they do not make Altium ERC validate the external harness.

Commissioning is still required

At the installed span, block each receiver completely with an opaque cover, disconnect each head and signal wire, and test car edges at different angles. Repeat with the neighbouring emitters powered: cross-talk must never produce CLEAR. Test glossy paint, chrome, oblique paths, lights, open-door daylight and vibration. A modulated sensor can still accept its own light reflected around an obstruction; modulation alone does not identify the optical path.

A static NPN output cannot detect every short to 0 V or stuck-ON failure. CLEAR means only that all configured sampled beam paths currently appear clear, not that every part of the car or door sweep has been checked. Require optical transition variation ≤ ±3 mm before considering it for a 10 mm clearance; otherwise change optics or move the safety plane back. Do not bypass the door's original safety sensors.
