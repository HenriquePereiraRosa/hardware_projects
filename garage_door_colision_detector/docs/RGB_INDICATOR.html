DarkNo valid clear indication. Check power, wiring and lamp.

Static binary sensor inputs cannot diagnose every sensor defect or a short-to-ground. This change does not add sensor proof-testing or lamp-current monitoring. A failed green driver could still create false green; do not treat this as a safety-rated indicator.

Recommended lamp

A 15 cm section of 12 V, non-addressable, common-anode 5050 RGB strip, 60 LEDs/metre, behind an opal diffuser in a small aluminium channel. Mount where it is directly visible from the driving position, not low beside a sensor or behind the car. Do not assume 15 cm is bright enough until tested through the closed windscreen in the brightest expected daylight.

Three 5 cm sections typically use about 60 mA per colour, 180 mA at white (~2.2 W). Confirm the purchased strip's specifications; different densities use different currents. The design load ceiling is 250 mA per channel, not a permission to connect a long roll. Begin at full brightness, then reduce PWM to avoid glare at night. The strip provides its own current-limiting resistors.

Documented strip option: Adafruit 346 · AliExpress search: 12V 5050 RGB 60 LED/m · Electrical/current reference. AliExpress is a search, not a vetted seller or guaranteed matching product. Do not buy WS2812/WS2815, 5 V addressable strip, RGBW, or a bare high-power RGB die for this connector.

This is not the old GREEN/RED/AMBER/GND connector. Follow printed strip pad labels, not wire colours. All three MOSFET sources connect to board GND. Each gate has 100 Ω series resistance and 100 kΩ gate-source pull-down. The ESP32 never supplies LED load current. Use a short local cable (target ≤1 m), restrained and insulated; longer runs need additional transient/EMC qualification.

CAD honesty: what is still missing?

The supplied ESP32 library has an imported symbol, footprint and complete board 3D model. Other schematic parts are electrical components with pins and intended part numbers, but their native footprint/STEP links are not finished. A named rectangle alone does not establish a purchasing or manufacturing-ready part.

Real KiCad SOT-23 footprint, STEP and AO3400A symbol-library data have been downloaded with provenance and licensing in hardware/altium/lib/upstream-kicad. They are not yet verified and linked in the Altium PCB library. The PCB was not changed. Optical heads remain unresolved because the exact through-beam sensor has not been selected. No invented optical-head 3D body is being presented as a real part.

Verification and handoff

The RGB-only update was executed and saved in native Altium: the UI sheet and two power-sheet net names changed. The UI resistors now use rectangular symbols with values inside. The beam sheet and PCB were untouched. The exported native connectivity audit passed 117 assertions, with 50 unique components and no shorts between named nets; this is not a full ERC or manufacturing sign-off. Backups are in hardware/altium/History/rgb-update-20260906. Firmware tests cover colour mapping and fault flashing, but PlatformIO/compiler execution is not available in the current shell. No flashed-hardware result is claimed.

Before relying on the lamp: check J5 with a meter; test all colours; reset and disconnect the ESP32; block every beam; unplug each receiver; cut controller power; verify the light is conspicuous through closed windows. Darkness and any unexpected display mean “do not rely on the detector”. Sensor boundary and reflection tests remain necessary.
