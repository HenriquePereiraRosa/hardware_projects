Confirmed preference and envelope

Proposed outer size: 100 mm wide × 100 mm deep × 600 mm high. Dimensions inside the cover will be smaller. This envelope is not yet checked against the garage or a complete CAD assembly.

Use rectangular resistor symbols, with the resistance value inside and the reference (R1, R2…) outside. Keep real electrical pins and the correct linked footprint. This preference is recorded; the saved Altium symbols have not been changed in this review.

Which face points across the garage?

The current candidate, Panasonic EX-L291, has emitter and receiver in one cable-connected head. Its optical face points at an RF-330 retroreflector on the opposite wall. Do not install two EX-L291 heads facing each other. A true through-beam design instead uses a matched transmitter and receiver.

The head mounts separately from the PCB. There are no PCB solder pins whose angle dictates its optical direction. Panasonic offers MS-EXL2-1 foot-angled, MS-EXL2-5 back-angled and MS-EXL2-4 adjustable brackets. Use the specified mounting plate where required. The -J pigtail option supports straight CN-24A-C2 or elbow CN-24AL-C2 mating cables; this changes cable routing, not the optical face. Manufacturer options and dimension drawings.

For any cheaper substitute, re-check its exact drawing. A PCB-mounted laser diode or bare receiver module is a different mechanical and electrical design; do not treat it as interchangeable.

Recommended physical arrangement

  1. Fix a rigid vertical backplate to the wall. Independently lock each head's pitch and yaw after alignment. Do not mount optics on the removable lid.
  2. Place the controller PCB parallel to the wall in a serviceable section of the enclosure. Use flexible sensor leads with strain relief; a long enclosure does not require a long PCB.
  3. Point optical faces across the opening on the car side of the complete moving-door envelope. Recess or guard the heads without clipping either outgoing or returning light.
  4. Keep cable bends, glands, terminal access, USB access and ESP32 antenna clearance in the mechanical model. Avoid an untested clear plastic window over the optics.
  5. Place the status lamp where the driver can see it, independently of the head direction. A smaller passive-side cover is acceptable.

Still needed before drilling: actual optical span, permitted wall protrusion, and the endpoints of the sketch's 43 mm dimension. A closed-door measurement alone does not describe the hinges and tracks during motion.

Finding a 600 mm enclosure

A cut-to-length cover/profile with end caps is a practical construction route. An inexpensive sealed enclosure in exactly 100 × 100 × 600 mm has not been verified. A useful alternative is a long protective optical cover plus a smaller gasketed electronics enclosure.

Legrand 636955, nominal 100 × 100 mm, 2 m length is a sourcing lead only: the page title says solid walls, but its description mentions side perforations. Confirm the actual construction, lid, end closures and local selling unit before buying. Slotted panel wiring duct is not a dust-resistant outer enclosure.

No IP54 claim is made for a cut profile. Gaskets, end closures, optical openings and cable entries all affect protection. A metal backplate/profile also requires deliberate ESP32 antenna placement.

Limits that mounting cannot fix

Keep EX-L291 provisional until the full-span repeatability and reflective-target tests pass. Use the matched retroreflector, not an ordinary plastic mirror. Discrete beams only test their own heights, not every point in a continuous plane.

Loss of signal must never produce green. However, the current single-output input does not detect every sensor defect: a stuck-on output or signal short to ground can mimic clear. Advisory only; no automatic door operation.
