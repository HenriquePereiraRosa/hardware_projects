Not ready for PCB manufacture or final purchasing

Only the ESP32 integrated library was supplied. Non-ESP32 footprints and STEP models remain pending and are labelled that way. Do not transfer the schematic to the old PCB or use the old BOM as an updated purchase list. Inherited hidden template metadata is not purchasing authority; use the explicit Manufacturer Part Number field and confirm the package before ordering.

Before layout: verify all remaining exact component packages and mappings, select matching socket headers for the development board, finish ERC and deliberate unused-pin treatment, and verify the prototype. The supplied board model does not by itself specify the socket stack height.

Power and fault behaviour

Remove JP1 before connecting USB power. Do not feed the DevKit from USB and its external 5 V header simultaneously. The sensor input expects NPN Light-ON operation: output sinks current only when the optical path is clear. Do not choose NC/NO solely by the label on a listing; verify its light/dark truth table.

Open receiver wiring, loss of receiver power and an interrupted beam all produce an unsafe input. A signal short to ground, stuck-on output, or some other sensor failures can falsely report clear; this circuit does not diagnose every defect. The optocouplers share system ground and therefore do not provide system-level galvanic isolation. Advisory only; do not use as a door safety interlock.

Recovery and editing

Original sheets and project are backed up in History/pre-library-repair-20260903/. The reproducible native script is scripts/RebuildRealSchematics.pas; running it replaces the three circuit sheets, so do not rerun after manual edits without making a fresh backup.

To inspect the ESP32 in 3D, open its PcbLib, click the canvas and press 3; press 2 for 2D. A schematic symbol is a functional drawing; its linked footprint contains the physical 3D body.
